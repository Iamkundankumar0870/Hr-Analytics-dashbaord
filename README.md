# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition and workforce trends. It helps HR teams understand employee turnover patterns and make data-driven decisions to improve retention and workforce management.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
- Analyze employee attrition trends.
- Identify factors influencing employee turnover.
- Support HR decision-making with actionable insights.
- Improve employee retention strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualization

---

## 📂 Data Source
- Source: Kaggle HR Analytics Dataset
- Dataset includes employee demographics, education, salary, job role, department, years at company, and attrition information.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- Employee Attrition Analysis
- Department-wise Filtering
- Attrition by Age, Gender, Salary, and Education
- Job Role-wise Attrition Tracking
- Employee Tenure Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard Visuals

---

## 📊 Key KPIs
- Total Employees: 1470
- Total Attrition: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Average Age: 37
- Average Salary: 6.5K
- Average Years at Company: 7

---

## 📈 Key Visuals
- KPI Cards for workforce metrics
- Attrition by Education (Donut Chart)
- Attrition by Age Group (Bar Chart)
- Attrition by Gender (Treemap)
- Attrition by Salary Range (Bar Chart)
- Attrition by Years at Company (Area Chart)
- Attrition by Job Role (Bar Chart)
- Job Role Attrition Matrix
- Department Filter/Slicer

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Employees aged 26–35 have the highest attrition.
- Employees earning up to 5K salary show the highest turnover.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition count.
- Sales Executives and Research Scientists are among the most affected roles.
- Most attrition occurs within the first few years of employment.
- Male attrition is higher than female attrition.
- Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds contribute the highest attrition.

---

## 🚀 Business Impact
- Helps HR teams identify high-risk employee segments.
- Supports employee retention and engagement strategies.
- Enables data-driven workforce planning.
- Assists management in reducing employee turnover.
- Improves hiring and retention decision-making.
- Converts HR data into actionable business insights.
